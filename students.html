Thank you for your interest in my group. I will join the University of Michigan as an Assistant Professor in Fall 2027 and am recruiting PhD students. Below are answers to the questions I am asked most often.

For Prospective PhD Students

Do you have PhD positions for Fall 2027?

Yes, I am actively looking for new PhD students. I am based in the Robotics Department, so I encourage you to apply to the University of Michigan Robotics PhD program; students in the CSE PhD program can also work with me. Please mention my name in your statement of purpose. Rest assured that your application will be considered even if you do not email me separately.

Should I email you before applying?

Emailing me before applying is not at all necessary—mentioning my name in your application is enough. If you do choose to email, rest assured that I will read your message, even if I am unable to reply. Please don’t be discouraged if you don’t hear back.

If you would still like to introduce yourself, email me with the subject line “[Prospective PhD – Michigan]”, attach your CV/resume, and briefly answer two questions:

  1. What kind of research do you want to pursue?
  2. Which research paper (mine or others’) do you find most exciting, and why?

For Current & Admitted Michigan Students

I am a current or admitted graduate student at the University of Michigan. Can I explore the possibility of joining your lab?

Yes! Please feel free to email me to set up a meeting.

I am a University of Michigan undergraduate. Are there opportunities to participate in your research? Are there any prerequisites?

Yes! Please feel free to email me with your CV, transcripts, and a brief statement of interest.
